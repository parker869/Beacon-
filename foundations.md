# FOUNDATIONS

## My portfolio
- Athena Alliance, sales and growth: choose the right next step and tone for each prospect so they feel seen and supported, never pressured.

## Decision rules

1. Personalize with skills and goals, not company names.
   - Example: For Joyce's referral email, I had the company names removed and kept her CPA and CFP credentials, her tax and equity expertise, and her speaking work.
   - Wrong answer: naming her employers or listing every job she has held.

2. When I say keep the CTA exactly as the original, do not reword it.
   - Example: For Kaitlyn's application follow-up, I asked for the CTA to stay word for word and only swapped the colleague's name.
   - Wrong answer: smoothing or "improving" the wording because it repeats a phrase.

3. A "not the right fit yet" reply leads with the person's accomplishments and never sounds exclusionary.
   - Example: For Khadija, I rewrote the email so it highlighted her strengths, framed the fit as timing, and closed by saying we look forward to supporting her in the future.
   - Wrong answer: listing the types of women the programming is built for.

## Escalation trigger
Stop and bring me in if a message involves health or private information about a prospect, or a commitment about pricing or terms.
